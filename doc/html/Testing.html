Testing

Every public unit carries seven test categories: contract, reference, precision, vectorization, robustness, metamorphic and speed. tests/auditTestCategories.m refuses a unit that is short of one, and the count is reported by the gate alongside the pass count.

Bounds are measured, never declared. A passing bound leaves its number in tests/last_run.txt so a later change can be compared against it, and a failing bound is decomposed rather than widened. Where cancellation governs, the bound is computed from eps of the quantities involved instead of written as a literal.

matlab -batch ciGate            %% the full gate
bash mex/test/runCChecks.sh     %% the C side, no MATLAB needed
python auditPackage.py          %% catalogue, help template, taxonomy, C2 scan
