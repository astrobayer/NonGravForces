\begin{abstract}
This document collects the theory of the non-gravitational accelerations and torques acting on low-Earth-orbit satellites, the practical knowledge needed to model them at the \SI{e-10}{\metre\per\second\squared} level for GRACE-FO accelerometer processing, and the implementation decisions of the \texttt{NonGravForces} toolbox: architecture, workflow, error handling, testing, performance, and the extension to future sensors (electrostatic, cold-atom interferometer, optomechanical, drag-free). It also records the audit of the two predecessor toolboxes and lists the test oracles every unit is verified against. Equations are given in the form the code implements; every approximation is named with its magnitude.
\end{abstract}
